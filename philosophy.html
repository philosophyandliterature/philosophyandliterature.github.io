The people behind the questions.

Philosophy is not only a history of doctrines. It is also a history of human beings who refused to accept the obvious — and asked what lies beneath it.

Featured thinker

Socrates

He left no writings, yet became one of the most influential figures in Western philosophy. His method was deceptively simple: keep asking why.

His legacy is not a finished system but an attitude towards thought — intellectual humility, questioning and examination of one's own life.
