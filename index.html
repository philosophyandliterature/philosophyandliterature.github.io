About

A place to think.

Philosophy & Literature is a growing digital archive for people who still enjoy reading slowly, questioning familiar ideas and finding meaning in difficult books.

It brings together philosophy, literature, books, quotations, essays and visual culture without pretending that every important question has a final answer.
